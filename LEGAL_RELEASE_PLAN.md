# Cozy Paint Studio Legal Release Plan

## Purpose

Prepare the website and Windows Store preview for a legally sound public release while keeping costs at zero until publication and minimizing recurring costs afterward.

This is a working implementation plan, not legal or tax advice. Requirements must be rechecked against the actual release build, Store configuration, and current law immediately before publication.

## Confirmed project facts

- Publisher: Johannes Vollmer, acting as one individual in Düsseldorf, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany.
- Product name: **Cozy Paint Studio**.
- The app is developed privately and part-time.
- Alpha and beta releases will be free.
- A later full release is intended to be paid at a consumer-friendly price, currently expected to be in the 5–30 EUR/USD range.
- The exact full-release price and public pricing copy remain undecided.
- The website is a static landing, support, privacy, and legal-information site.
- The website will not process purchases or provide paid downloads.
- Paid distribution will happen only through external app stores.
- Website domain: `cozy-paint-studio.johannesvollmer.com`.
- Support email: `cozy-paint-studio@johannesvollmer.com`.
- Email provider: IONOS Basic Mail.
- Website host: GitHub Pages.
- No website analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, contact forms, third-party fonts, or embedded social media are planned.
- Ordinary support correspondence may be retained for up to 12 months after the matter is closed, unless it is needed longer for legal claims or statutory retention obligations.

## Cost policy

Do not activate paid address or telephone services while the product and website remain private and unpublished.

Expected minimum costs near launch:

- Gewerbeanmeldung in NRW: one-time fee of approximately €26 for an individual.
- Privacy-preserving Impressum address: approximately €6.90 per month if Anschrift.net is selected.
- Separate virtual telephone number: approximately €1–4 per month, if required.
- ELSTER tax registration: no fee.
- Domain and email: already active costs.

There is no completely free method to publish a legally required physical address while keeping the home address private. Until a compliant address is active, the safe choices are to keep the site non-public or publish the home address. The preferred approach is to keep the site private.

## Release phases

### Phase 1: private development

- Keep the website and downloadable app non-public.
- Do not activate the paid address service yet.
- Do not activate a separate telephone service yet.
- Do not register the Gewerbe solely for private development.
- Draft and test the legal pages with conspicuous release-blocking placeholders.
- Do not deploy a page containing an incomplete public Impressum.

### Phase 2: release candidate

Begin this phase once there is a real public preview build and a planned publication date.

1. Verify the release build's storage, deletion, backup, export, sharing, networking, telemetry, crash-reporting, and Store-update behavior.
2. Select and activate the privacy-preserving address service.
3. Obtain written confirmation that the selected address arrangement is suitable for the intended legal and administrative uses.
4. Decide whether a virtual telephone number will be published.
5. Register the Gewerbe as a part-time individual business immediately before public commercial operation begins.
6. Complete the ELSTER tax-registration questionnaire within the applicable deadline.
7. Finalize the German and English legal pages with real, verified facts.
8. Run a final legal, privacy, link, accessibility, and clean-device review.

### Phase 3: public preview

- Publish only after every release blocker below has been resolved.
- Keep Store and website language clear that the preview is free and pre-release.
- Do not imply that Android, web, or other planned platforms are currently downloadable.
- Ensure that all public product, publisher, support, privacy, and Store information agrees.

### Phase 4: paid full release

Before enabling a paid Store offer:

- Finalize the price and pricing copy.
- Complete the Partner Center payout and tax profiles.
- Complete the appropriate US tax form, normally W-8BEN for an individual German publisher.
- Confirm with the Finanzamt or a tax professional whether a VAT ID and cross-border reporting are required for the applicable Microsoft contracting entity and payout model.
- Recheck the privacy notice, Store policies, app behavior, and consumer information.
- Keep the website informational unless a deliberate decision is made to introduce direct sales.

## Gewerbe and business structure

The intended minimal structure is an unregistered individual sole proprietorship (`Einzelunternehmen`/`Kleingewerbe`) operated as a secondary occupation (`Nebengewerbe`). No GmbH or voluntary Commercial Register entry is planned.

Suggested activity description:

> Entwicklung, Veröffentlichung und Vertrieb von Software, insbesondere einer digitalen Malanwendung.

The Gewerbe should be registered no later than the start of paid distribution. Because a public free preview supports a continuing product with an explicit later profit intention, confirm with the Düsseldorf Gewerbemeldestelle whether it considers the public-preview date the start of commercial operation. Register at that point if advised.

The Gewerbe registration is made under the proprietor's legal first and last name. `Cozy Paint Studio` remains the product name and must not be presented as a separate legal entity.

Do not use `e.K.`, GmbH, or another legal-form suffix unless that status is actually obtained.

## Address privacy

The public legal notice requires a real street address suitable for legal contact and service. A PO box, Packstation, or ordinary mail-forwarding address is insufficient.

Anschrift.net is the likely low-cost choice but is not yet contracted. Before purchase, obtain written confirmation that the selected plan provides:

- Use in an Impressum under § 5 DDG and § 18 MStV.
- Acceptance of formal legal service and registered mail.
- Use by an individual sole proprietor.
- Immediate notification and digital access to received mail.
- A staffed physical address and real workspace/use right.
- Clear rules for address use after cancellation and forwarding of late-arriving legal mail.
- Permission for relevant business correspondence and registrations, where needed.

Ask Anschrift.net and the Düsseldorf Gewerbemeldestelle:

> Ich entwickle als Einzelperson nebenberuflich eine Software und möchte die Anschrift für das Impressum nach § 5 DDG sowie für förmliche Zustellungen nutzen. Die tatsächliche Geschäftsleitung und Entwicklung befinden sich in Düsseldorf. Kann die Anschrift als öffentliche Impressumsanschrift genutzt werden, während das Gewerbe in Düsseldorf unter meiner nicht veröffentlichten Wohnanschrift angemeldet bleibt?

Do not assume that a remote address automatically becomes the correct business establishment for the Gewerbeanmeldung. Avoid creating an unintended registration in another municipality or a later fee for relocating the business.

## Telephone privacy

Do not publish the private telephone number.

Shortly before release, either:

1. Activate a separate German VoIP/business number with forwarding, voicemail, schedules, outgoing caller ID, and caller blocking; or
2. Obtain legal confirmation that the published electronic contact method provides sufficiently rapid, direct, and effective communication without a telephone number.

A separate low-cost number is the simpler and more defensible option for a static site without a contact form. Candidate providers include easybell, fonial, and sipgate. Verify current prices, cancellation terms, number portability, address verification, forwarding charges, and abuse-blocking controls before ordering.

## Minimum German Impressum

The final German Impressum should contain only applicable facts:

- Heading and legal basis.
- Full legal name: Johannes Vollmer.
- Valid public street/business address.
- Country: Germany.
- Support email address.
- Separate business telephone number, if selected.
- A short clarification that Cozy Paint Studio is the product name of software offered by Johannes Vollmer.
- VAT ID or Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer only if one has actually been issued and must be published.
- Register, supervisory-authority, or regulated-profession information only if actually applicable.
- Consumer-dispute information if applicable or deliberately included for clarity.

Never publish the ordinary personal German tax number.

Do not link to the discontinued EU Online Dispute Resolution platform.

A separate responsible person under § 18(2) MStV is only needed if the site becomes a journalistically/editorially designed publication. The current product landing page and personal founder story do not appear to require that additional designation, but this must be reconsidered if a news, editorial, or regularly published article section is added.

## Minimum privacy notice

### Controller

Publish:

- Johannes Vollmer.
- The same valid public address used for the legal notice.
- `cozy-paint-studio@johannesvollmer.com`.
- The separate business telephone number, if selected.

The competent German supervisory authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia (`Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen`, LDI NRW).

### Website and GitHub Pages

Explain that:

- GitHub Pages hosts the static website.
- GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes.
- Technical request information may include IP address, request time, requested path, browser/device information, referrer, and response status.
- Processing is necessary to securely deliver the website and is based on the controller's legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- GitHub may process data outside the EEA using its published transfer safeguards.
- The website itself uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, contact form, third-party fonts, or embedded social-media content.
- No consent banner is needed while no non-essential cookie, local-storage, analytics, advertising, or comparable technology is added.

### Support email and IONOS

Explain that:

- IONOS SE operates the Basic Mail mailbox.
- Email data may include the sender's address and name, message content, technical headers, attachments, and voluntarily supplied diagnostic information.
- The purpose is handling support, privacy, legal, and other correspondence.
- Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where correspondence concerns a prospective or existing user relationship.
- Article 6(1)(f) GDPR applies to general correspondence, service improvement, documentation, and abuse prevention.
- Messages are not used for advertising.
- Ordinary support correspondence is deleted no later than 12 months after the matter is closed unless legal claims or statutory retention obligations require longer storage.
- Users should not send private artwork unless it is explicitly requested and they voluntarily choose to do so.
- The IONOS account and processing agreement must be reviewed before publication to confirm that the description matches the active Basic Mail contract.

### Desktop application

Verify and then describe:

- Whether an account is required.
- Which paintings, settings, logs, thumbnails, backups, and application data are stored locally.
- Exact storage locations.
- How users back up, export, and delete their data.
- What happens to data on uninstall.
- Whether the app itself makes any network request.
- Whether telemetry, analytics, crash reports, licensing, or update checks exist.
- Whether Microsoft receives reliability or Store-related information independently.
- Which actions deliberately transfer data through operating-system sharing or export features.
- Which links open the system browser.

Do not publish absolute claims such as "no data leaves the device" until they have been checked against the packaged Store build and Windows/Store behavior.

### Microsoft Store and external links

Explain that:

- The website does not process purchases or paid downloads.
- Microsoft Store is an independent external service.
- Data is transferred to Microsoft only when a visitor follows a normal Store link or uses Microsoft services.
- Store acquisition, licensing, installation, updates, payments, and reliability processing are governed by Microsoft's applicable terms and privacy information.
- Discord and any other external destination remain optional third-party services.
- No embeds or preconnections to Store, Discord, or social platforms may be added without revising the privacy assessment.

### Rights

Include, where applicable:

- Access.
- Correction.
- Deletion.
- Restriction.
- Data portability.
- Objection, especially to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- Withdrawal of consent if consent-based processing is introduced later.
- Complaint to the LDI NRW or another competent supervisory authority.
- A statement that no automated decision-making or profiling occurs, if accurate.
- A statement that no data-protection officer has been appointed because one is not legally required, if accurate.

## Terms, sales, and consumer information

The website currently needs no checkout terms, payment policy, withdrawal page, or refund page because it does not sell or license the app directly.

Microsoft Store manages Store checkout and acquisition. This does not remove the publisher's business, tax, privacy, or product-information duties.

Do not create generic custom terms unless a concrete need arises, such as:

- Direct website sales.
- User accounts or hosted services.
- A custom licence replacing or supplementing Store terms.
- Subscriptions or in-app purchases outside Store commerce.
- Paid downloadable brush packs.
- Community uploads or a hosted gallery.

## Publication blockers

The website and public preview must not launch until all of the following are true:

- [ ] A valid public service/business address has been activated and contractually verified.
- [ ] The address arrangement has been checked with the relevant provider and, if necessary, the Düsseldorf Gewerbemeldestelle.
- [ ] The timing of the Gewerbeanmeldung has been confirmed.
- [ ] The Gewerbe has been registered if public operation has begun or the authority requires it for the public preview.
- [ ] A separate telephone number or legally sufficient alternative direct-contact method is active.
- [ ] The final address and contact data appear in both German and English legal pages.
- [ ] GitHub Pages hosting and IONOS Basic Mail processing are accurately described.
- [ ] Packaged-app storage, deletion, backup, networking, telemetry, crash-reporting, Store, and uninstall behavior have been verified.
- [ ] The privacy notice matches the actual release build.
- [ ] No unresolved legal placeholders remain in production output.
- [ ] The public domain, support mailbox, privacy page, legal notice, and Store destination work over HTTPS.
- [ ] All external links have been checked immediately before release.
